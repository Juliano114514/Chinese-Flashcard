Narration: 32 fixed clips generated with the existing pinned MeloTTS FP32 model; repeated context, duration crop, amplitude adjustment and edge fade. No runtime synthesis or download. No human listening certification. Model/code licenses: chinese-speech/LICENSES.txt.
